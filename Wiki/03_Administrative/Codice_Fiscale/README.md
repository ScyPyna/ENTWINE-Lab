# Codice fiscale

The *codice fiscale* is the Italian tax code. You will need it for almost
everything else: the residence permit, an Italian bank account, the national
health service, a phone contract, and your contract with the university.

## How to get it

**There is no need to go to the Agenzia delle Entrate.**

Ask Gianni to email Paola Ciancio and Gianpiero Barbuto at the central UNICAL
offices: they will handle the request for you.
